scope: owned launch process for a 40-person org

THE MESS

Product launches were handled via scattered threads and varying templates. Ownership diffused, deadlines missed, launches slipped 6+ weeks.

THE CALL

Killed all status meetings. One living doc, one weekly 20-minute sync, disagreements resolved in comments before the room.

THE SYSTEM

async team updates → living doc + 20-min sync → one exec-visible roadmap

RESULT

Next launch shipped in 5 weeks end-to-end. The process still runs without me in the room.
